## At a glance

- We serve **B2B buyers** (brands, distributors, gyms/retailers, online sellers).
- We collect **business contact and project details** you submit.
- We use data to **answer OEM, wholesale, and sample requests**.
- We do **not sell** your data to data brokers.
- Privacy requests: [Contact](/contact) with subject **Privacy Request**.

## Who we are

**Crest Creatine** (“we”) operates this website for creatine OEM / private label and wholesale inquiries.

## Data we collect

- Name, email, company/buyer type, looking-for, volume, and message content
- Technical data such as IP, browser, and pages visited (if analytics is enabled)
- Lead attribution fields if present (UTM / referrer)

## How we use data

To respond to quotes, samples, and account requests; improve the site; and meet legal obligations.

## Sharing

We may use processors (hosting, form/database, email, analytics). We do not sell personal information.

## Retention & rights

We retain inquiry records as needed for business follow-up. You may request access or deletion via the contact form.
